Build-time sounds
=================

Place WAV, MP3, or OGG files in this directory before building the app.
They are embedded into the executable and appear together with built-in and
runtime-imported sounds.

Filename aliases select defaults when the corresponding setting has never been
saved:

- alert, overtime, просрочка: overtime alert;
- questions, время вопросов: transition to questions;
- next, следующий докладчик: transition to the next speaker.

All other supported files are still included in the sound list.
